WebRTC is on: the demo is also downloaded with WebTorrent from other players who have this page open, and while you play, your browser shares it with them in turn (at most 1 MB/s). Network games connect the players' browsers directly, so players can see each other's IP addresses. WebRTC needs this page on https (or localhost): here, the demo comes from this site, and network games go through its server (if it can pass them on).

About the demo

The game files downloaded here are only those of the official Serious Sam: The First Encounter demo (2001), which, to our knowledge, is freely redistributable. It is the same demo that was given away on magazine discs and download sites and that the Internet Archive hosts today. Game files from your own installation, your settings and your saved games stay on your device: they are never shared.
